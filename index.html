Decide what moves.

Bookmark Sorter keeps the review step in your hands. Live sorting is optional and starts off by default.

01

Write a rule

Match a bookmark by its domain, URL, or title and choose an exact destination folder.

02

Preview the moves

Review the planned changes before applying them. If a bookmark changes after preview, the sorter skips it.

03

Sort with a way back

Apply the plan when you are ready. An Undo journal helps reverse supported moves and cleanup changes.

Bookmark processing stays in your browser.

Bookmark titles, URLs, folders, rules, previews, and Undo history are processed locally. The extension asks for bookmark and local storage permissions. It does not use advertising or analytics.

Free use does not contact the licensing service. Pro activation will send the license key and random installation identifiers for license checks, never bookmark contents.
