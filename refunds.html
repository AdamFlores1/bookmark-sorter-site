Refunds and cancellation

Last updated 2026-10-08.

These pages describe the planned launch offer. Purchases are not open.

For the planned Pro launch, you can request a refund for Bookmark Sorter Pro within 30 days of purchase. Email adamjohnflores@gmail.com with the receipt order number and checkout email. A reason is welcome so we can understand the problem, but is not required for a request within this period.

Do not include your full license key, card details or bookmark data. We aim to respond within three business days, verify the purchase, and arrange the refund through Dodo Payments. Dodo processes the refund to the original payment method. Bank or payment-provider processing time can vary.

After a refund

A fully refunded license no longer authorizes Pro. Online checks remove Pro access when the license is revoked; an offline installation may retain its previously signed entitlement until its offline period ends. Your rules and bookmarks stay in your browser. Free features remain available.

For a partial refund, we will explain what happens to the key before arranging it. Dodo may revoke license grants when a one-time payment is refunded; we do not promise that a partially refunded key will remain usable.

No subscription to cancel

Pro is a one-time purchase. There are no recurring charges to cancel. Deactivating an installation frees a license slot; it does not itself request a refund. Optional support sent through another platform follows that platform's terms and does not buy Pro.

Consumer rights

This 30-day policy is additional to applicable statutory rights, including remedies for faulty or misdescribed digital content. It does not restrict any right of withdrawal that applies to your purchase. For digital content supplied immediately, any legally required consent or acknowledgement belongs in checkout; merely using this site does not waive your rights.
