Support and contact

IOANNIS FLOROS maintains Bookmark Sorter. Contact adamjohnflores@gmail.com for support and privacy questions.

Describe the issue

  • Include your Chrome and extension versions.
  • Describe what you tried and the visible error message.
  • Use a small synthetic example if a rule does not match as expected.

Avoid sending bookmark exports, passwords or payment details. Remove private bookmarks from screenshots. Before troubleshooting changes, export a backup and inspect any pending recovery record.

Support the project

Optional donations support development and unlock nothing. The external platform handles the visit and any donation.
