Support and contact

For setup, activation, lost keys, refunds or privacy questions, email adamjohnflores@gmail.com. We aim to reply within three business days.

Help us locate the problem

  • Include the Chrome and extension versions.
  • Describe the action you tried and the visible error message or code.
  • For a purchase or lost key, include the receipt order number and checkout email.

Never send a full license key, payment-card details, passwords or bookmark export. If a screenshot helps, remove private bookmarks and keys first.

Lost key or installation

Once purchases open, we will verify the purchase against Dodo's order records. Recovery information is sent to the verified purchasing email. If you cannot reach an old installation, describe that problem so we can review its activation slot. Proof of purchase is required before account or license changes.

Optional project support

Optional support on Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee supports development. It does not unlock Pro and is handled separately from Dodo license purchases.
