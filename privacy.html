Bookmarks stay in your browser

The extension reads bookmark titles, URLs and folders to preview and apply your rules, review exact-URL duplicates, export backups and support Undo. It keeps rules, preferences, activity and recovery records in Chrome's local extension storage. Live sorting is off until you enable it.

Bookmark contents, rules, previews and Undo history are not sent to our licensing service or payment provider. You choose when to export a backup or rules file and where to keep it. Chrome's own bookmark-sync behavior is controlled by your Chrome settings and Google's policies.

Permissions and Pro activation

The bookmarks permission enables bookmark operations. Storage retains settings, history and local license state. Free use does not require contact with the licensing service or its optional host permission. The extension has no advertising or analytics services and does not generate a hardware fingerprint.

If you activate Pro, the extension requests permission for the exact licensing host. Activation sends your key, a random installation ID and credential, and the extension version. Later checks send installation credentials and the current token ID. Deactivation sends installation credentials. The entered key is not retained by the extension; a signed entitlement and random installation credentials are stored locally.

Licensing, checkout and service providers

The prepared licensing service is designed to use Cloudflare Workers and D1, and Dodo Payments for key activation, validation, deactivation, and signed delivery/revocation events. It stores provider license/payment identifiers, encrypted keys, keyed lookup fingerprints, random installation IDs, hashed credentials, provider instance IDs, request records and revocation references. It does not store your bookmarks or customer email in D1.

The planned Dodo checkout will collect the customer, billing, tax and payment information needed at checkout. We will access relevant purchase records for support and refunds through its merchant dashboard. Dodo acts as the legal seller and an independent controller for its payment processing. See Dodo's privacy policy. We do not receive card numbers through the extension or licensing database.

This prelaunch website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub's privacy statement. The planned licensing service uses Cloudflare; its application rate-limit identifiers are keyed hashes. The website loads its fonts and images locally and uses no marketing trackers or contact form. Support email is handled through Gmail.

Purposes and lawful bases

We use licensing and purchase references to perform the Pro license contract and provide support. We use limited security records to protect the service and prevent abuse, based on legitimate interests. Records required by law are retained to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Our use of Chrome API data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and history remain until you clear them or remove the extension. Exports remain where you save them. Deactivating Pro removes the current entitlement, but does not erase every local installation record.

The prepared licensing code schedules daily cleanup. Completed or failed requests and processed or ignored webhooks become eligible after 90 days. Deactivated installations become eligible 90 days after deactivation. Revoked licenses and installations become eligible after 90 days when a durable revocation reference exists. Cleanup is bounded to 500 rows per category per daily run, so a backlog can take longer. Active licenses and unresolved operations remain while needed. Revocation references remain to prevent a refunded or disabled key from being reactivated.

Production log and backup retention will be confirmed before sales.

Support emails are kept while handling your request and for necessary follow-up or legal obligations. Contact us to request removal of information no longer needed. Payment records have Dodo's separate retention rules. A deletion request that erases an active license can end Pro access; we will explain that before proceeding.

Security and international processing

Pro requests use HTTPS. Stored keys are encrypted, installation credentials are hashed, and the extension verifies signed entitlement tokens. Service providers may process data outside Greece or the EEA. Their applicable transfer safeguards and privacy information are described in their policies. Contact us for information about the safeguards used for your data.
